What's this blue-haired girl's business?

Hatsune "Humberto Roberto Alonzo Santiago" Miku, born in 1908 and hailing from Acre, Brazil, is a girl who comes from absolutely no musical background at all. In her upbringing, she was never surrounded by things that encompassed the idea of “modern living”, as she was a particularly rural girl who coexisted alongside dinosaurs and stuff (because those still exist in Acre). As she would turn older, her whole life would turn around. After moving to The Bronx in New York City, she would meet with musician A Boogie wit da Hoodie for the first time. While spending time with him, Miku would open herself up to a whole world of music that she would have previously considered unfamiliar. After a while, she would come to decide that it would be really awesome and cool and special and fantastic and amazing and spectacular if she made music too. She took absolutely no inspiration from A Boogie wit da Hoodie in this decision, however. She really liked him as a friend, but thinks his music sucks and is bad and awful and really just outright horrible (she liked a few songs off of his ‘Artist’ album, however, but that’s it). In her musical upbringing, Miku would take a fond interest in things of a tacky nature. Her interest in low quality things would be accompanied by a liking for general MIDI music, especially songs performed with Roland GM/GS soundfonts. Her musical premise would ultimately be a combination of tackiness as an aesthetic and musical execution through said MIDI instruments. Through this premise, she would form "Miku's Little Toy Band", with members consisting of no one but her and her instruments. She hopes to one day perform on top of the Pacific Ocean just cause.
